Ensemble

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Ensemble, a personal project by Amit Harel

In short

Ensemble is software one person runs on his own Mac, for himself. It reads his mail, his WhatsApp messages and his calendar so it can tell him what needs him. It does not sell data, show ads, or share data with anyone except the services listed below, which it needs to work.

Who uses it

Ensemble is not offered to the public. Its only user is its owner. If anyone else ever signs in to it, everything in this policy applies to their data too.

What it reads and why

SourceWhat it readsWhat it does with it
GmailMessages, their senders, subjects and labels (Google scope gmail.modify)Sorts mail into labels, notices mail that needs a reply or holds a request, turns requests into the owner's tasks, and searches and reads the mailbox when the owner asks. It sends, replies to, forwards or relabels mail only when the owner approves each one, and it never deletes mail.
Google DriveOnly files Ensemble itself creates (scope drive.file)Uploads a report of each sorting run (which mail went to which label) and a backup of the sorting database, to the owner's own Drive.
Google CalendarThe owner's eventsShows the day's events, checks which shows he plays, and proposes changes — which happen only after he approves each one.
WhatsAppMessages in the chats and groups the owner chooses to includeNotices dates, changes and requests about his work, and tells him about them.

Where the data is kept

On the owner's own Mac, in local files and a local database, with run reports and database backups in his own Google Drive. Access tokens and keys are kept in the Mac's Keychain. The owner can delete any of it at any time.

Who else sees it

To understand a message, Ensemble sometimes sends its text to an AI model. Passwords, one-time codes, keys and tokens are always removed first and never leave the Mac. In mail it reads on its own, it also removes long numbers (card, account, ID and phone numbers). Mail the owner asks for himself — a search, a mail he wants read — is sent as it is, apart from passwords, codes, keys and tokens. Mail text can contain anything its senders wrote, health details included. The services involved are:

  • Anthropic (Claude) and OpenRouter (the classifier models it routes to) — to judge what a message asks, through their APIs, under their terms for API data.
  • Composio — the connection Ensemble uses to reach Gmail (searching and reading mail, and sending only with the owner's approval) and Google Calendar. Composio holds its own sign-in to the owner's Google account.
  • Telegram — carries Ensemble's messages to the owner's own chat.
  • Google and WhatsApp — the sources themselves.

Some of the work is done by a model that runs on the Mac itself; that text never leaves it.

Google user data

Ensemble's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above to the owner. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train general AI models, and not read by people other than the owner.

Keeping and deleting

Data stays until the owner deletes it. Revoking Ensemble's access in the Google account settings (Security → Third-party apps) stops all further reading of Gmail and Drive at once.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: github.com/amit-harel.
