Ensemble is a small band of AI agents that runs on a Mac at home. It reads the mail, the WhatsApp groups and the calendar of a working musician, and brings him only what needs him — a show that moved, a request to answer, a date to confirm.

Built by Amit Harel, a keyboard player and producer, for his own working life.

It listens where the work already happens.

Nobody has to move into a new app. Each source has one part that reads it and knows its rules; what concerns him reaches one screen and one chat.

What it will not do.

Change anything without your tap.

A moved show, a new date, a message to send: each one is a card that says exactly what will happen. Approve it, and the receipt stays.

Ring you for small talk.

A show someone else covers, a summary, a chat between friends: it stays quiet.

Forget what you told it.

Every “not needed” and every correction is kept, so it learns what matters to you.

It stays home.

Ensemble runs on one Mac, for one person. Mail is sorted there and the records stay there. Only the words a model must read to judge a message leave it, and some things never do.
